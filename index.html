Meet the owner

I spent 18 years in the tire business before I moved Munroe’s to 3430 Main Street in Hartford. If it’s your first time coming by, look for the yellow sign above the bay. That’s us.

Maybe you’ve put air in the same tire twice this week. Maybe you need a replacement and want to know what it’ll cost before payday. Those are good questions to bring through our door. We can check where a tire is leaking, explain whether it can be repaired, or walk you through new and used options in your size.

You don’t need to know the name of a part to start a conversation here. Just tell us what’s happening with your car. We’ll take it from there.

Our tire shop in Hartford, CT

Look for the yellow Munroe’s Tire sign at 3430 Main Street. Inside the bay, we carry both new and used tires. Call us with the size on your tire’s sidewall and we’ll check what’s on the rack before you make the trip.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have used tires in my size?

We carry new and used tires. Call us at (860) 246-0886 with the size printed on the side of your tire, like 205/55R16, and we’ll check what we have.

How much does a replacement tire cost?

Our tires start at $50 for 14–16-inch wheels, $60 for 17–19-inch wheels and $80 for 20–22-inch wheels. Give us your tire size and we’ll quote you a price with installation.

Can you fix a flat tire?

Yes. We’ll check where your tire is leaking and let you know if we can repair it. If it needs a plug, we charge $20.

Can you mount and balance my tires?

Yes. We charge $15 to mount a tire on a 14–17-inch wheel and $20 for larger wheels up to 22 inches. We also offer speed balancing for $15.

How long should I plan for a tire change?

It depends on how many tires you need changed and how busy we are. Give us a call before you head over and we’ll tell you what kind of wait to expect.
